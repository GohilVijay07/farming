About AgriConnect Gujarat

Empowering Gujarat's agrarian community across Saurashtra, North, Central, South Gujarat, and Kutch with modern digital crop records, micro-irrigation management, and district weather advisories.

Dedicated to Gujarat

Bridging Gujarat's Agrarian Heritage with Modern Technology

AgriConnect is built specifically for the farming community of Gujarat, India. Gujarat is a national leader in cotton, groundnut, spices, castor, dairy, and horticulture.

From the black cotton soils of Saurashtra to the fertile Goradu tracts of Charotar and the dryland farms of Kutch and Banaskantha, AgriConnect equips every Gujarati farmer with digital record-keeping, GGRC drip irrigation guidelines, and localized 33-district weather forecasting.

5 Agro-Climatic Zones of Gujarat

  • Saurashtra: Groundnut (મગફળી), Cotton (કપાસ), Sesame, Mahuva Onion, and Talala Kesar Mango.
  • North Gujarat: Unjha Cumin (જીરું), Mustard, Fennel, Castor (દિવેલા), and Deesa Potato.
  • Central Gujarat & Bhal: Bhalia Wheat, Tobacco, Paddy, Maize, and Dairy Fodder.
  • South Gujarat: Sugarcane (શેરડી), Paddy, Banana, Chikoo, and 100% Organic Dang.
  • Kutch: Date Palm, Pomegranate, Castor, Bajra, and Dryland Horticulture.
