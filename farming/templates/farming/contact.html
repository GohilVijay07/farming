Frequently Asked

Is AgriConnect free for farmers?

Yes, AgriConnect is 100% free to register, manage crops, and view agricultural tips.

Can other farmers see my crop data?

No, each farmer account is completely isolated. You only have access to your own crops and farm area records.

How do I update my crop status?

Go to "My Crops" from your dashboard and click the "Edit" action button next to the desired crop.
